Friends of Green Lake Crew

We stay in the boat for the rowers who come after us.

FOGLC is the volunteer heart behind Green Lake Crew — alumni, parents, coaches, and neighbors who believe a seat in a boat should never depend on what you can afford.

Our Mission

Rowing changed us — the early mornings, the boat that only moves when everyone moves together, the coach who believed in us before we believed in ourselves. Friends of Green Lake Crew exists so that feeling doesn't end when our own oars come out of the water.

We are the community that shows up behind the program: raising the resources that put a scholarship rower in a boat, keep the fleet safe and race-ready, train the coaches our athletes trust, and build a home on Green Lake that will outlast all of us. We believe every athlete who wants to row should get the chance to, regardless of what they can pay, what their body can do, or how far Green Lake feels from the life they're building.

This is our way of staying in the boat — long after our own seat is someone else's.

— Friends of Green Lake Crew

What We Believe

A few things guide everything FOGLC does, on and off the water.

Access first

Cost should never be the reason a rower isn't in the boat. Scholarships come first.

Safety and stewardship

Well-maintained boats and trained coaches aren't optional — they're how we protect every athlete on the lake.

Room for every rower

Adaptive rowing isn't an afterthought. Everyone who wants to race deserves the chance to.

Built to last

From stem to stern, and from our current boathouse to our future Launch House, we invest for the next fifty years of rowers, not just this one.
